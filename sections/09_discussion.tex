\section{Discussion and Limitations}
\label{sec:discussion}

The two campaigns establish different parts of the joint problem. The PowerDynamics experiments show that eight voltage/current PMUs can reconstruct the requested 31-bus voltage field accurately under the tested nominal perturbations even though the 114-state local model is far from full rank. The ANDES experiments show that the same sensor buses support strong abnormal-event detection and moderate known-source localization across eight event labels. Neither result implies the other: low state TVE does not guarantee source separation, and high detection F1 does not imply a correct hidden state or physical source.

Functional observability and source diagnosability explain the distinction. Proposition~\ref{prop:functional-reconstruction} removes state directions that do not affect the requested voltage function. Proposition~\ref{prop:source-indistinguishability} instead asks whether two interventions remain different after uncertain initial state is projected out. These are local linear results. They do not certify a nonlinear DAE over large faults, changing topology, or unmodeled controllers. Their scientific value depends on a prospective empirical bridge: the functional residual should predict reconstruction error, and pairwise information should predict candidate-set size, posterior entropy, or time to source resolution.

The first bridge has partial evidence. The preregistered functional residual correlates with bus-level error, but the exceptions show that excitation, numerical tolerance, estimator covariance, and electrical distance still matter. The second bridge remains untested. The source-held-out collapse is consistent with poor physical separation or source memorization, but the present artifacts cannot distinguish those explanations. Computing \eqref{eq:pairwise-information} before training and comparing it with held-source errors is therefore more informative than adding another classifier layer.

The state-adaptation result narrows the role of identifiability. On M6 operating-point shift, the physical static MAP closes \EZeroHClosureOverall{} of the oracle-center gap although only \EZeroHRank{} of \EZeroHDimension{} nuisance directions are locally informed. Accurate recovery of a target function can coexist with non-unique nuisance coordinates. This does not extend automatically to line outages, faults, or sequential event-conditioned estimation. The successful solver uses one equilibrium snapshot, and its Laplace covariance remains severely under-dispersed in the joint NEES-like diagnostic.

The legacy event baseline identifies both reusable mechanisms and a hard failure. Multiscale temporal summaries, adaptive residuals, and simultaneous PMU contrasts improve known-source physical ranking by \LegacyPhysicalGainPP{} percentage points. Availability should remain an explicit integrity input because a missing measurement is observed directly. By contrast, source identities and endpoint descriptors permit memorization: with complete physical-source holdout, exact localization falls to \HeldSourceRate{}. A stronger hybrid should therefore compute engineered features on candidate physical residuals, $\bm y-\widehat{\bm y}_h$, and compare them with a physics-only likelihood. This reuses domain knowledge without treating training assets as the candidate universe.

Five limitations block a submission-ready joint-method claim. First, the state and event evidence use different simulators and are not an end-to-end common-contract comparison. Second, the PowerDynamics plant and RAW0001 are simulated; no field or hardware-in-the-loop validation is included. Third, the event bank has short, fixed-onset trajectories and about 7.15~min of normal-labeled exposure per split, which is insufficient for operational false-alarm claims. Fourth, PMU loss/join has not been tied prospectively to the information measure, and topology/source transfer remains unvalidated. Fifth, the proposed posterior in \eqref{eq:hypothesis-posterior} has not been calibrated; classifier votes and uncorrected evidence scores must not be called probabilities.

The next frozen campaign should use one audited plant and measurement operator across all four baselines: learned-only, physics-only, physics plus learned residual discrepancy, and diagnosability-aware inference with abstention. Complete event parents and physical sources must remain disjoint across training, development, calibration, and test. The protocol should vary operating point, parameter mismatch, PMU loss/join, and at least one simulator; expose a long normal stream; report false alarms per hour, event recall, joint event-plus-source correctness, candidate-set coverage and size, source-resolution delay, TVE, NLL/ECE, runtime, and failed solves. Until that campaign is complete, this manuscript is a consolidated scientific draft with validated components and an explicit falsifiable gap, not a completed demonstration of joint superiority.
