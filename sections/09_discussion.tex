\section{Discussion and Limitations}
\label{sec:discussion}

The three contracts expose different parts of the inverse problem. Eight voltage/current PMUs recover the requested 31-bus voltage field accurately in the PowerDynamics state tests even though the 114-state local model is not fully observable. A separate physical load-response dictionary supports normalized event and source evidence on the same simulator. The ANDES bank measures what the earlier supervised representation retained when source labels were removed. Low hidden-voltage HVRE still does not guarantee source separation, and high event visibility does not guarantee exact localization.

The local theory explains this split. Proposition~\ref{prop:functional-reconstruction} removes unobservable directions that do not affect the requested voltage function, and Corollary~\ref{cor:functional-blue} attaches a covariance to that estimable function. Source diagnosis has a different geometry. Proposition~\ref{prop:source-indistinguishability} identifies intersecting candidate--nuisance response sets. Proposition~\ref{prop:relative-contact} then prevents a stronger but invalid inference: zero first-order separation need not imply nonlinear impossibility because relative curvature can leave second-order evidence. Conversely, a positive affine margin is certified only while the nonlinear remainder stays below it. Large faults, topology switches, and unmodeled controls may violate both local descriptions.

Two theory-to-data links receive preliminary tests. The functional residual correlates with bus-level voltage error, but its projector fails rank monotonicity and needs an SVD/noise-tied recomputation; excitation, covariance, and electrical distance also confound it. Amplitude Fisher information is associated with the source-wise detection threshold, with the expected sign. Six threshold magnitudes over 16 sources make this exploratory. The pairwise Fisher quantity was compared with pooled 16-way confusion, a mismatched test rather than evidence against the pairwise theory. The new relative-curvature result is verified only by an algebraic toy check; evaluating it on IEEE 39-bus candidates requires separately estimated nuisance tangent and curvature.

The operating-point study also separates target recovery from parameter recovery. Regularized physical recentering closes \EZeroHClosureOverall{} of the oracle-center gap on M6 although only \EZeroHRank{} of \EZeroHDimension{} nuisance directions are locally informed. The hidden-voltage target can therefore be accurate while the injection coordinates remain non-unique. This result is confined to an equilibrium snapshot. It does not cover line outages, faults, or sequential event-conditioned estimation, and the local curvature covariance remains under-dispersed in the joint NEES-like statistic.

The legacy event model supplies one reusable mechanism and one warning. Multiscale summaries, adaptive residuals, and simultaneous PMU contrasts improve known-source physical ranking by \LegacyPhysicalGainPP{} percentage points. Availability belongs in the integrity state because a missing measurement is observed directly. Source identities and endpoint descriptors, however, allow memorization. Exact localization falls to \HeldSourceRate{} under complete physical-source holdout. The load experiment avoids an asset-label head by evaluating each candidate as a physical intervention. Learned residual features may still help, but their contribution must be measured against this physics-only likelihood.

The principal evidence gap is a multi-family end-to-end experiment. The three studies have different targets or simulator semantics and are simulated. The Bayesian result covers one family, fixed topology, known onset, closed candidates, matched operation, and synthetic noise; it does not fit state/integrity nuisance. The ANDES records have fixed onsets and about 7.15~min of normal exposure per split. Neither field validity nor operational false-alarm control is established.

A decisive follow-up compares learned-only, physics-only, learned-residual, and diagnosability-aware methods under one audited plant and PMU operator. Event parents and sources must remain disjoint across fitting, calibration, and test. Frozen shift, topology, and PMU-availability regimes plus long normal exposure must report joint correctness, candidate-set coverage, delay, false alarms per hour, HVRE, calibration, runtime, and failed solves. Until then, multi-family superiority remains unproved.
