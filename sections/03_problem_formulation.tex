\section{Dynamic PMU Network and Problem Formulation}
\label{sec:problem-formulation}

\subsection{Dynamic Network and Physical Interventions}

Let $\bm{x}(t) \in \R^{n_x}$ denote dynamic states, $\bm{z}(t) \in \R^{n_z}$ algebraic states, and $\bm{u}(t)$ exogenous inputs. We model the power system over an observation horizon by
\begin{align}
  \dot{\bm{x}}(t) &= \bm{f}\bigl(\bm{x}(t),\bm{z}(t),\bm{u}(t),\bm{a}(t);\bm{\theta}\bigr), \label{eq:dae-dynamic}\\
  \bm{0} &= \bm{g}\bigl(\bm{x}(t),\bm{z}(t),\bm{u}(t),\bm{a}(t);\bm{\theta}\bigr), \label{eq:dae-algebraic}
\end{align}
where $\bm{\theta}$ collects network and component parameters. The event variable $\bm{a}(t)$ encodes a physical intervention rather than a learned source label. A candidate can be written as
\begin{equation}
  a = (c,\ell,\tau,\gamma,\ldots), \label{eq:intervention}
\end{equation}
where $c$ is the family, $\ell$ is the affected bus, line, generator, or load, $\tau$ is onset time, and $\gamma$ is a severity or magnitude parameter. The initial candidate families are faults, line outages, generation changes, and load changes. The hypothesis space also includes a no-physical-event reference $H_0$.

A bus, a line, and a PMU are distinct objects. A bus or line may be the physical locus of an intervention; a PMU is a measurement device whose location and channels determine a projection of the network state. This distinction is necessary when a line event is inferred from remote voltage and current phasors.

\subsection{PMU Observations and Integrity States}

At sampling index $k$, let $\Pset_k$ be the PMUs currently available to the inference system. For PMU $p\in\Pset_k$, the observation model is
\begin{equation}
 \bm{y}_{p,k} = \mathcal{M}_{p,m_{p,k}}\!\left(\bm{h}_p(\bm{x}_k,\bm{z}_k)\right) + \bm{v}_{p,k}, \label{eq:pmu-measurement}
\end{equation}
where $\bm{h}_p$ selects or transforms state variables into the local voltage and current phasors, frequency, and rate of change of frequency; $\bm{v}_{p,k}$ is measurement noise; and $m_{p,k}$ is a measurement-integrity state. The operator $\mathcal{M}_{p,m_{p,k}}$ may describe a nominal channel, a missing sample, bad or corrupted data, a communication loss, or recovery. Availability and quality indicators are therefore observations about the measurement process, not alternate values of $a$.

The reporting set may vary as $\Pset_k \ne \Pset_{k+1}$. A PMU joining enlarges the observed projection, whereas a departing PMU removes one. The formulation does not require a fixed ordering or a fixed number of PMU channels. It instead records the identity, observed channels, and integrity state of every member of $\Pset_k$.

\subsection{Inference Objective and Decisions}

Let $\Hset=\{H_0,H_1,\ldots,H_J\}$ contain the physical hypotheses generated from candidate families, locations, onset times, and admissible magnitudes. The central target is the causal posterior
\begin{equation}
 p\!\left(H_j \given \mathcal{Y}_{1:k},\mathcal{I}_{1:k},\Pset_{1:k}\right), \qquad H_j\in\Hset, \label{eq:target-posterior}
\end{equation}
where $\mathcal{Y}_{1:k}$ contains observations through $k$ and $\mathcal{I}_{1:k}$ contains integrity information through $k$. A system that reports a causal decision at $k$ may not use samples from $k+1$ or later to form \eqref{eq:target-posterior}.

The decision layer maps this posterior and a diagnosability assessment into one of three outputs: an exact source, a candidate set, or an abstention. For a declared physical family $c$, a simple candidate set has the form
\begin{equation}
 \mathcal{C}_{c,k} = \left\{H_j\in\Hset_c : p(H_j\given\mathcal{Y}_{1:k},\mathcal{I}_{1:k},\Pset_{1:k}) \geq \eta_{c,k}\right\}, \label{eq:candidate-set}
\end{equation}
with an operating threshold $\eta_{c,k}$ specified and validated in the experimental protocol. An exact claim such as \emph{FAULT @ BUS17} requires singleton, adequately supported evidence. A set such as \emph{FAULT @ \{BUS17, BUS18\}} preserves source ambiguity. If neither a unique source nor a useful set is justified, the correct output is \emph{PHYSICAL EVENT --- EXACT SOURCE UNRESOLVED}.
