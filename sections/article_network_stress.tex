\subsection{Network Mismatch}
A separate test replayed 108 interventions from 18 physical schedules
under three network conditions and two noise scales. With their original
thresholds, the five-model and nominal banks produced \COneMixTimely{}
and \COneNomTimely{} timely attributions across \COneMixTotal{} event
realizations. When the resistance and reactance perturbations fell
outside the five-model bank, it emitted 68 unmatched alarms in 1.08
monitored event-hours and failed to issue a timely diagnosis on four interventions.
The other four network/noise combinations together produced four extras
and no missed deadlines. Neither bank raised an alarm during 11.8
monitored normal hours per method. The supplement gives the condition
breakdowns and the persistent-line comparison.
