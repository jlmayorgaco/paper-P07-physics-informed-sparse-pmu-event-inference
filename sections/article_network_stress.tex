\subsection{Sensitivity to Network Mismatch}

The independent evaluation repeated 108 interventions in 18 parents under
six network and noise conditions. The five-model bank correctly attributed
\COneMixTimely{}/\COneMixTotal{} before OFF and the nominal bank
\COneNomTimely{}/\COneNomTotal{}. Of the five-model bank's
\COneMixExtras{} unmatched alarms, 68 occurred under the network
perturbation outside the model set, as did its four late or omitted
diagnoses. Neither physical bank alarmed in 11.8 monitored normal hours
per method. The supplement gives the family breakdown, condition-specific
counts and the comparisons at original operating points.
