\section{Recoverability and Diagnosability Under Sparse PMUs}
\label{sec:functional-observability}

\subsection{Functional Observability of the Requested Voltage Field}

Linearizing the index-reduced DAE around a verified equilibrium and sampling at $\Delta t=1/30$~s gives
\begin{align}
 \delta\bm x_{k+1} &= \bm A_d\delta\bm x_k+\bm w_k, \label{eq:local-state}\\
 \delta\bm y_k &= \bm C\delta\bm x_k+\bm v_k, \label{eq:local-output}\\
 \delta\bm z_{\mathcal U,k} &= \bm L_{\mathcal U}\delta\bm x_k. \label{eq:local-target}
\end{align}
The reduction assumes a regular local algebraic Jacobian after the reference mode is fixed. Here $\delta\bm x_k\in\mathbb R^{114}$ contains reduced differential coordinates, $\bm C$ maps them to 32 rectangular voltage/current PMU channels, and $\bm L_{\mathcal U}$ maps them to 62 rectangular hidden-voltage components. These matrices are local and hypothesis dependent after a topology change.

For a horizon of $N$ transitions, define
\begin{equation}
 \mathcal O_N=\operatorname{col}\left(\bm C,\bm C\bm A_d,\ldots,\bm C\bm A_d^N\right). \label{eq:observability-matrix}
\end{equation}
Full-state observability requires $\operatorname{rank}(\mathcal O_N)=114$. The requested voltage at time $r\leq N$ instead has map $\bm L_r=\bm L_{\mathcal U}\bm A_d^r$. Recovering the complete local target sequence uses $\mathcal L_N=\operatorname{col}(\bm L_0,\ldots,\bm L_N)$.

\begin{proposition}[Finite-horizon functional reconstruction]
\label{prop:functional-reconstruction}
For the noise-free local system, the target at time $r$ is uniquely determined by the output sequence through $N$ if and only if
\begin{equation}
 \ker\mathcal O_N\subseteq\ker\bm L_r. \label{eq:functional-condition}
\end{equation}
The complete target sequence is uniquely determined if and only if $\ker\mathcal O_N\subseteq\ker\mathcal L_N$.
\end{proposition}

\IEEEproof
Two initial states produce the same output history exactly when their difference belongs to $\ker\mathcal O_N$. Their requested outputs at time $r$ agree for every such pair exactly when the difference also belongs to $\ker\bm L_r$. Stacking the maps gives the sequence statement. Equivalently, the rows of the requested map lie in the row space of $\mathcal O_N$.
\endIEEEproof

This standard functional-observability result \cite{fernando2010functional,zhang2025functional} fixes the target and time index before any estimator is assessed. It also shows why a low full-state rank alone does not preclude recovery of the hidden-voltage function. In a nonlinear DAE, however, a favorable kernel inclusion at one equilibrium is only first-order evidence: local functional factorization requires a constant-rank neighborhood condition on the feasible observation fibers \cite{montanari2022functional}.

The Gauss--Markov specialization adds a variance statement to the rank condition. Stack the output perturbations as $\delta\bm Y_N=\mathcal O_N\delta\bm x_0+\bm v_N$, assume $\bm v_N\sim\mathcal N(\bm0,\bm R_N)$ with $\bm R_N\succ0$, and define $\bm G_N=\mathcal O_N^{\mathsf T}\bm R_N^{-1}\mathcal O_N$.

\begin{corollary}[Minimum-variance functional estimate]
\label{cor:functional-blue}
If \eqref{eq:functional-condition} holds, the best linear unbiased estimate of the time-$r$ target and its error covariance are
\begin{align}
 \widehat{\delta\bm z}_{\mathcal U,r}
 &=\bm L_r\bm G_N^{\dagger}\mathcal O_N^{\mathsf T}
   \bm R_N^{-1}\delta\bm Y_N, \label{eq:functional-blue}\\
 \bm\Sigma_{z,r}^{\star}
 &=\bm L_r\bm G_N^{\dagger}\bm L_r^{\mathsf T}. \label{eq:functional-blue-cov}
\end{align}
Every other linear unbiased estimator has covariance
$\bm\Sigma_{z,r}^{\star}+\bm Q\bm R_N\bm Q^{\mathsf T}$ for some $\bm Q$ satisfying $\bm Q\mathcal O_N=\bm0$.
\end{corollary}

\IEEEproof
Condition \eqref{eq:functional-condition} implies $\bm L_r=\bm L_r\bm G_N^{\dagger}\bm G_N$, which proves unbiasedness of \eqref{eq:functional-blue}. Direct substitution gives \eqref{eq:functional-blue-cov}. Any other unbiased linear map is the displayed map plus $\bm Q$ with $\bm Q\mathcal O_N=\bm0$. The covariance cross terms vanish because $\mathcal O_N^{\mathsf T}\bm Q^{\mathsf T}=\bm0$, leaving the positive-semidefinite term $\bm Q\bm R_N\bm Q^{\mathsf T}$.
\endIEEEproof

For whitened measurements, $\operatorname{tr}(\bm\Sigma_{z,r}^{\star})$ quantifies noise amplification in the requested voltage field. It depends on singular values and target alignment, not rank alone. The numerical curve below is an implementation of this quantity with a tolerance-dependent pseudoinverse; it is a conditioning diagnostic rather than an exact certificate.

\subsection{Numerical Audit for the Prescribed Eight-PMU Case}

For the anchor-time target used by the structural audit, define
\begin{align}
 \bm\Pi_N&=\mathcal O_N^{\dagger}\mathcal O_N, \label{eq:observable-projector}\\
 r_F(N)&=\left\|\bm L_{\mathcal U}(\bm I-\bm\Pi_N)\right\|_F, \label{eq:functional-residual}\\
 r_{F,i}(N)&=\left\|\bm L_i(\bm I-\bm\Pi_N)\right\|_F. \label{eq:bus-functional-residual}
\end{align}
A zero residual is an exact numerical condition only when the matrix scaling and rank decision are defensible. A nonzero value certifies that the computed observable subspace does not contain every requested anchor-time direction.

The implementation formed $\mathcal O_N^{\mathsf T}\mathcal O_N$ and retained eigenvalues above $10^{-9}$ of its maximum. Figure~\ref{fig:observability-horizon} turns the audit into a claim test. The pseudo-rank reaches 54 at $N=30$ but falls to 39 at $N=180$, although the exact rank of a vertically augmented observability matrix cannot decrease. Gramian squaring, unstable-mode scaling, and a relative threshold therefore make this pseudo-rank unsuitable as an algebraic certificate. Longer history still lowers the trace-style noise bound by more than five orders of magnitude, but $r_F(N)$ remains nonzero. The evidence supports improved numerical conditioning, not full-state or exact target observability. Empirical voltage recovery is evaluated separately and is not used to repair the failed certificate.

Horizons $N\in\{0,3,10,30,60,120,180\}$ correspond to 0--6~s. The nonlinear nominal trajectories contain 91 frames, or 3~s, so $N=180$ is not a matched causal horizon for those records. Its bus ranking was nevertheless exported before nonlinear scoring and is retained only as a preregistered, cross-horizon numerical diagnostic. The empirical test correlates $r_{F,i}(180)$ with bus-level HVRE; because both derive from the threshold-sensitive projector, that association is exploratory until an SVD-based, noise-tied rank audit is frozen. It does not convert the six-second local audit into a three-second observability proof.

\subsection{Event-Source Diagnosability From Candidate Manifolds}

State recoverability and source diagnosability have different nuisance spaces. The construction below specializes established descriptor-model distinguishability \cite{eriksson2013diagnosability}, matched-subspace nuisance profiling \cite{scharf1994matched}, and fault/attack identifiability \cite{nyberg2002detectability,pasqualetti2013attack} to candidate-specific DAE responses and explicit integrity supports. The contribution is the common PMU problem contract and its separation from the requested-state problem, not the underlying projection identity. Under candidate $h$, stack the available channels over $H$ samples as
\begin{equation}
 \bm Y_H=\bm s_{h,H}+\mathcal O_{h,H}\bm\xi_h
 +\mathcal E_{S,H}\bm\beta_h+\bm\varepsilon_H,
 \qquad \bm\varepsilon_H\sim\mathcal N(\bm0,\bm R_H), \label{eq:stacked-hypothesis}
\end{equation}
where $\bm s_{h,H}$ is the intervention response, $\bm\xi_h$ is the locally admissible initial-state deviation, and $\mathcal E_{S,H}\bm\beta_h$ is corruption on a fixed support $S$. Allowing each candidate to refit nuisance variables produces the affine mean set
\begin{equation}
 \mathcal M_h(S)=\bm s_{h,H}+\operatorname{col}[\mathcal O_{h,H},\mathcal E_{S,H}]. \label{eq:hypothesis-manifold}
\end{equation}
This candidate-specific form is required for topology-changing events because $\mathcal O_{i,H}$ and $\mathcal O_{j,H}$ need not agree.

Let $\Delta\bm s_{ij}=\bm s_{i,H}-\bm s_{j,H}$ and
\begin{equation}
 \mathcal N_{ij,S}=[\mathcal O_{i,H},-\mathcal O_{j,H},\mathcal E_{S,H}]. \label{eq:pair-nuisance}
\end{equation}
The $\bm R_H^{-1}$-orthogonal nuisance projector and the pairwise separation margin are
\begin{align}
 \bm P_{ij,S}^{\perp}&=\bm I-\mathcal N_{ij,S}
 (\mathcal N_{ij,S}^{\mathsf T}\bm R_H^{-1}\mathcal N_{ij,S})^{\dagger}
 \mathcal N_{ij,S}^{\mathsf T}\bm R_H^{-1}, \label{eq:nuisance-projector}\\
 D_{ij,S}^{(\mathcal P)}(H)&=\frac12
 \left\|\bm P_{ij,S}^{\perp}\Delta\bm s_{ij}\right\|_{\bm R_H^{-1}}^2. \label{eq:pairwise-information}
\end{align}
Equation~\eqref{eq:pairwise-information} is half the minimum squared weighted distance between the two affine manifolds. It becomes the Gaussian mean-separation term only after nuisance values are fixed at the closest points. If all candidates are conditioned on one common pre-event state, the corresponding model replaces the two independent state blocks in \eqref{eq:pair-nuisance} by $\mathcal O_{i,H}-\mathcal O_{j,H}$. The analysis below uses independent candidate-specific state blocks; it does not use the common-state alternative.

\begin{proposition}[Local composite-hypothesis indistinguishability]
\label{prop:source-indistinguishability}
If $D_{ij,S}^{(\mathcal P)}(H)=0$, then $\mathcal M_i(S)$ and $\mathcal M_j(S)$ intersect. Hence there exist admissible nuisance values under candidates $i$ and $j$ that induce the same Gaussian measurement law. No decision rule can distinguish the pair uniformly over the declared nuisance set; with equal prior mass on those two parameter points, its average error is at least $1/2$.
\end{proposition}

\IEEEproof
The margin is zero exactly when $\Delta\bm s_{ij}\in\operatorname{col}(\mathcal N_{ij,S})$, which is equivalent to an intersection of the two affine mean sets. At the intersecting nuisance values, the means and covariance are identical. The observation law is therefore identical under both parameter points, so the two conditional error probabilities sum to one.
\endIEEEproof

When the corruption support is unknown but at most $q$ channels are affected, the conservative margin is
\begin{equation}
 D_{ij}^{(q)}(H)=\min_{S:\,|S|\le q}D_{ij,S}^{(\mathcal P)}(H). \label{eq:sparse-integrity-margin}
\end{equation}
This union-of-subspaces formulation connects source localization to attack identifiability, sparse strong observability, and unknown-input reconstruction \cite{pasqualetti2013attack,hou1998input,showkatbakhsh2020secure,sun2026secure}. It also states the condition required for distinguishability under sparse corruption.

\begin{corollary}[Monotonicity under an added PMU]
\label{cor:pmu-additivity}
For the same pair, covariance model, nuisance specification, and integrity budget, adding a conditionally independent PMU block cannot reduce the profiled margin:
\begin{equation}
 D_{ij,S}^{(\mathcal P\cup\{p\})}(H)\ge D_{ij,S}^{(\mathcal P)}(H). \label{eq:information-additivity}
\end{equation}
\end{corollary}

For every nuisance vector, the augmented squared residual is the old nonnegative residual plus the new PMU residual. Taking the minimum preserves the inequality. Equality with a sum of separately profiled margins does not hold in general because the same nuisance variables must explain both sensor blocks; additivity requires fixed simple hypotheses or additional separability conditions.

The affine margin tests only first-order separation. To state what a zero margin does and does not imply, work in whitened coordinates and let the local no-event explanations and one event path be
\begin{align}
 \bm F_0(\bm b)&=\bm N\bm b+\tfrac12\bm H_0[\bm b,\bm b]+O(\|\bm b\|^3),\nonumber\\
 \bm c_g(a)&=a\bm d_g+a^2\bm q_g+O(a^3).
 \label{eq:relative-contact-model}
\end{align}
Define $\Delta_g(a)=\min_{\bm b\ \mathrm{local}}\|\bm c_g(a)-\bm F_0(\bm b)\|_2$. Here $\bm b$ parameterizes a regular observable nuisance chart, so redundant coordinates have been removed and $\bm N$ has full column rank. The construction applies pairwise after one candidate manifold is used as the local reference.

\begin{proposition}[Relative order of distinguishability]
\label{prop:relative-contact}
Let $\bm P_\perp=\bm I-\bm N(\bm N^{\mathsf T}\bm N)^{-1}\bm N^{\mathsf T}$. If $\bm P_\perp\bm d_g\ne\bm0$, then
\begin{equation}
 \Delta_g^2(a)=a^2\|\bm P_\perp\bm d_g\|_2^2+O(a^3). \label{eq:first-order-contact}
\end{equation}
If $\bm d_g=\bm N\bm v$, define the relative curvature
\begin{equation}
 \bm\kappa_g=\bm P_\perp\!\left(\bm q_g-\tfrac12\bm H_0[\bm v,\bm v]\right). \label{eq:relative-curvature}
\end{equation}
Then $\Delta_g^2(a)=a^4\|\bm\kappa_g\|_2^2+O(a^5)$. Thus zero first-order information does not imply local nonlinear indistinguishability when $\bm\kappa_g\ne\bm0$.
\end{proposition}

\IEEEproof
The stationarity equation has derivative $\bm N^{\mathsf T}\bm N\succ\bm0$ in $\bm b$, so the implicit-function theorem gives a smooth minimizer. With $\bm v_*=(\bm N^{\mathsf T}\bm N)^{-1}\bm N^{\mathsf T}\bm d_g$, expanding $\bm b_*(a)=a\bm v_*+a^2\bm w+O(a^3)$ leaves $a\bm P_\perp\bm d_g$ at first order. If $\bm d_g=\bm N\bm v_*$, profiling removes the tangential quadratic term and leaves $a^2\bm\kappa_g+O(a^3)$; squaring proves the claim.
\endIEEEproof

For $n$ independent Gaussian windows, the closest-null divergence is $n\Delta_g^2(a)/2$; Pinsker's inequality bounds level-$\alpha$ power by $\alpha+\min\{1,\sqrt n\Delta_g(a)/2\}$. Hence contact order $r$ gives limiting amplitude scale $n^{-1/(2r)}$: $n^{-1/2}$ at first order and $n^{-1/4}$ at second order \cite{rotnitzky2000singular}. This result is local and mode preserving. Correlated frames enter through their joint covariance, not as independent replications.

A positive affine margin has a complementary guarantee. If each exact candidate response has a radius-$r$ remainder bounded by $\kappa_h\|\bm\eta_h\|_2^2/2$, the reverse triangle inequality gives $d_{ij}^{\mathrm{nl}}(r)\ge[d_{ij}^{\mathrm{lin}}-(\kappa_i+\kappa_j)r^2/2]_+$. Neither this bound nor Proposition~\ref{prop:relative-contact} is certified for the multi-family bank; the load study measures only source-wise Taylor remainder.

The executed load-intervention case gives a narrower matched-signal specialization. Let $\bm D_g$ be the whitened unit-amplitude response for candidate source $g$ and let $\bm\Sigma$ be the normal-data covariance before whitening. The amplitude Fisher information is
\begin{equation}
 \mathcal I_g=\bm D_g^{\mathsf T}\bm\Sigma^{-1}\bm D_g, \qquad
 |a|_{\min}=\frac{z_{1-\alpha}+z_{1-\beta}}{\sqrt{\mathcal I_g}},
 \label{eq:event-visible-information}
\end{equation}
where the second expression is the local amplitude required by a one-sided Gaussian score with false-alarm probability $\alpha$ and power $1-\beta$. The formula predicts detectability, not source identity.

For source $j$ as the closest single-signature alternative, the information left after its amplitude is fitted is
\begin{equation}
 \mathcal J_{g\rightarrow j}=\mathcal I_g-
 \frac{(\bm D_g^{\mathsf T}\bm\Sigma^{-1}\bm D_j)^2}
 {\bm D_j^{\mathsf T}\bm\Sigma^{-1}\bm D_j}
 =\mathcal I_g(1-\mu_{gj}^{2}),
 \label{eq:projected-fisher}
\end{equation}
with $\mu_{gj}$ the covariance-weighted signature coherence. Equation~\eqref{eq:projected-fisher} is the profiled pairwise Fisher information for one signature against another. It need not predict multicandidate confusion once amplitude priors, unequal alternatives, and finite nonlinear response are present.

For a calibrated source posterior, define the smallest candidate set satisfying
\begin{equation}
 \mathcal C_k(\alpha)=\argmin_{\mathcal C\subseteq\mathcal H}
 \left\{|\mathcal C|:\sum_{h\in\mathcal C}p(h\mid\bm y_{0:k})\ge1-\alpha\right\}. \label{eq:candidate-set}
\end{equation}
A diagnosability-aware system abstains from exact Top-1 when $|\mathcal C_k|>1$ or when its closest competing margin falls below a development-set threshold. The load-intervention study tests only the amplitude-information relation in \eqref{eq:event-visible-information} and reports an exploratory, mismatched aggregate comparison for \eqref{eq:projected-fisher}. It does not fit the initial-state nuisance $\mathcal O_h\bm\xi_h$, corruption nuisance $\mathcal E_S\bm\beta_h$, or the curvature constant $\kappa_h$. Candidate-set calibration, source-resolution delay, and PMU loss or addition still require a source-disjoint multi-family experiment.
