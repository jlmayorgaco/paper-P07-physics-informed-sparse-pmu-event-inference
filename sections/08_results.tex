\section{Uncertainty, Physical Mismatch, and Adaptation}
\label{sec:results}

\subsection{Marginal Calibration Does Not Whiten Innovations}

The nominal B2 covariance is highly conservative: U0 covers essentially all real and imaginary errors at the nominal 95\% level. Fitting two temperatures on 30 independent CAL trajectories improves TEST hidden NLL from $-13.477$ to \UtwoNLL{} and gives \UtwoCoverageRe{} and \UtwoCoverageIm{} marginal 95\% coverage for real and imaginary components, respectively. This is useful marginal calibration, but it leaves the innovation lag-one autocorrelation unchanged at \OriginalInnovationACF{} and the Ljung--Box test rejects whiteness. Figure~\ref{fig:temporal-uncertainty}(b)--(c) and Table~\ref{tab:uncertainty-results} therefore separate interval coverage from temporal model adequacy.

The calibration residual is strongly low rank: its first two principal components explain 95.7\% of variance and four explain 99.2\%. That empirical fact does not guarantee a useful generative correction. The rank-four measurement Gauss--Markov model reduces lag-one autocorrelation from \OriginalInnovationACF{} to \MeasurementGMInnovationACF{}, but worsens TVE, produces hidden NLL 14.991, and undercovers both components. The rank-one process placement leaves both TVE and autocorrelation effectively unchanged. These ablations reject the claim that low-rank colored PMU residuals, inserted without stronger physical structure, improve hidden-state uncertainty.

\subsection{Which Physical Mismatches Matter?}

The 980-case rebuilt-plant campaign reveals sharp family dependence (Fig.~\ref{fig:physical-mismatch} and Table~\ref{tab:mismatch-results}). At $m=1.5$, network mismatch reaches \NetworkMismatchRatio{}$\times$ the pooled nominal median and operating-point mismatch reaches \OperatingMismatchRatio{}$\times$. The coupled family is smaller at 1.602$\times$, while machine, governor, AVR, and load-model families remain near the nominal median for the tested perturbations. A blanket claim of robustness would therefore be misleading: the estimator is insensitive to several tested component shifts but fails materially when the algebraic network map or equilibrium center changes.

Nominal covariance repair does not transfer automatically. Averaged across all mismatch cases, U0 has \MismatchUzeroCoverage{} 95\% coverage and nominally fitted U2 has only \MismatchUtwoCoverage{}. The reduction reflects physical shift, not a calibration/test leak: U2 remains frozen from nominal CAL data. This result limits the interpretation of Section~\ref{sec:estimation}: nominal dispersion scaling is not robustness to a changed plant.

\subsection{Adequacy Is Detectable, but the Tested Adaptation Fails}

The observed-PMU innovation statistic detects many cases in which hidden TVE is unacceptable. Macro AUROC/AUPRC are \AdequacyHalfAUROC{}/\AdequacyHalfAUPRC{} at $m=0.5$ and \AdequacyMaxAUROC{}/\AdequacyMaxAUPRC{} at $m=1.5$ (Fig.~\ref{fig:adequacy-adaptation}(b)). Undefined family-level AUROCs, caused when every frame has the same frozen adequacy label, are omitted from the macro mean; AUPRC remains reported. Consequently, the statistic supports model-adequacy triage, not mismatch-family classification or guaranteed alarm calibration.

\begin{figure*}[t]
\centering
\includegraphics[width=\textwidth]{figures/generated/adequacy_adaptation.pdf}
\caption{Failure-mechanism and adaptation evidence. (a) Median TEST TVE at $m=1.5$ for the frozen estimator, evaluation-only oracle center, and causal observed-PMU offset proxy. (b) macro model-adequacy discrimination using a five-frame trailing Mahalanobis innovation statistic fitted only on nominal CAL data.}
\label{fig:adequacy-adaptation}

Oracle recentering changes the conclusion from ``insufficient PMUs'' to ``stale local physics.'' At $m=1.5$, it reduces network, operating-point, and coupled median TVE from \NetworkFrozenTVE{}, \OperatingFrozenTVE{}, and \CoupledFrozenTVE{} to \NetworkOracleTVE{}, \OperatingOracleTVE{}, and \CoupledOracleTVE{}, respectively. Oracle relinearization closes the remaining gap in the small diagnostic subset. The causal offset-only proxy, however, gives \NetworkOnlineTVE{}, \OperatingOnlineTVE{}, and \CoupledOnlineTVE{} and closes a median \OnlineClosurePercent{} of the oracle gap over nonnominal identifiable cases. Its nominal-safety gate passes, but its recovery gate fails. Observed-PMU offset fitting is therefore not an adequate substitute for leakage-safe AC recentering and local model rebuilding.
