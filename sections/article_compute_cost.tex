\subsection{Processing Time}
In the controlled two-plant comparison, the median recorded runtime
of the history arm for a 120-s event trajectory was 37.4\,s on the reference plant
and 49.2\,s on the second plant (36 trajectories per plant). The mean and reset
arms took 46.6 and 46.3\,s on the reference plant and 48.1 and 48.3\,s on the second plant.
The timer aggregates processing across each record and cannot resolve
individual report latencies.

The five-model bank in the separate network-mismatch evaluation took
a median 358.7\,s of single-thread inference per 120-s event or
persistent-line record, or 99.6\,ms per report at 30 reports/s.
Constructing the prefix-specific bank took a further median 80.1\,s.
In the six-record noise replay, the nominal bank alone took
\RevNomMsReport{}\,ms per report at the median and
\RevNomMsReportMax{}\,ms at the maximum of the record averages.
The five-model inference stage exceeded the 33.3-ms inter-report
interval even on an amortized basis.
