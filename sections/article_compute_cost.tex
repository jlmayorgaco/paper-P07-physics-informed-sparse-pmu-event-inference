\subsection{Computational Cost}
\input{tables/generated/closure_compute_cost}
Table~\ref{tab:closure-compute-cost} records the stages of the evaluation
campaign, which ran the five-model bank on one thread. Its history
inference takes a median 358.7\,s per 120-s record (99.6\,ms per report),
three times slower than the 30-report/s input, so the five-model bank as
implemented is an offline causal replay. The cost is dominated by the
number of network hypotheses. Re-running the unchanged nominal bank alone
on the same hardware and thread settings (Section~\ref{sec:noise-stress})
gives \RevNomMsReport\,ms per report (median over \RevNomRecords{}
records, maximum \RevNomMsReportMax\,ms), below the 33.3-ms report
interval; the nominal bank attributed \COneNomTimely{}/\COneNomTotal{}
interventions in the independent evaluation. These are amortized
processing rates on a desktop CPU; per-report latency percentiles and
memory were not instrumented, and the 80-s startup bank construction is a
one-time cost. Decision timestamps elsewhere in the paper are report times,
not wall-clock latency.
