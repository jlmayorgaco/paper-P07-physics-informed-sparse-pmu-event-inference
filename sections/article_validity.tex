\label{sec:validity}

\emph{Plant family.} The bank was developed and scored on simulations of one plant family. On an independent plant the static attributor keeps its accuracy and the learners lose up to \SPDrop{} sources, but the bank itself was not run there. Its advantage over the other methods could shrink or grow on another plant, and this is the largest open point of the evidence.
\emph{Few independent units.} The held-out cohort has 12 event records and the confirmatory simulation 18 parents. A method that is right in all 12 records is bounded below by 74\% at 95\% confidence, not by the event-level count.
\emph{Reuse of the held-out records.} They were opened by the bank, by two static attributors and, once each, by the learners of this section. No learner or threshold was tuned on them, but they are no longer a pristine test for the physical methods.
\emph{Competition record.} It is a validation set with logged iterations, and it contains one load change and one line outage among its twelve events. The bank does not start on it, so the competition record does not test the bank.
\emph{Event duration.} The simulated interventions last 2--5\,s, while the labeled load changes of the record last minutes. The bank is evaluated where recovery dominates, and persistent changes are covered only by the static attributor.
\emph{Noise.} Independent per-PMU noise of \RevNoiseAddedRmsLow{}\% added RMS TVE degrades load attribution. Estimating cross-PMU covariance from the startup prefix is a candidate extension that was not tested.
\emph{Baseline strength.} The learners saw 72 training events, and more noise draws did not close the gap. A learner given far more physical variety could do better, so the comparison measures what the available data support.
