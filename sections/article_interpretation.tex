\subsection{What Recovery Memory Establishes}
A separate full-DAE control supplies the earlier history and later onset.
Source separation stays essentially unchanged when that history is known
and falls when prior severity is uncertain; binary source accuracy has no
mean gain. Retaining recovery is therefore justified as a consistent
prediction of the continuing response, not as a universal increase in
location information. The stream comparison tests the complete frozen
procedure. Its admission-rule ablation does not isolate every operation
that retains recovery.

\subsection{What the Comparison Does Not Establish}
Trajectory libraries, sequential likelihoods and multiple-model filters
precede this work. The claim here is the specified competition of new
interventions with retained recovery, and its measured behavior, rather
than a general filtering optimum. Warner's trajectory procedure was not
run on this cohort. The feature baseline's trailing summaries describe
how signals changed, but do not encode the counterfactual response of
\emph{each candidate source} through the network and PMU terminal map.
That is a concrete difference in information used by the algorithms and
is consistent with the observed load gap; these experiments do not prove
that a better feature set or larger training set could not close it.
The original hackathon submission addressed nine labels with 30-s
decisions. It is a useful earlier result and a source of feature design,
but its internal validation score cannot be compared numerically with
event-level timely attribution here. The two causal ExtraTrees adapters
were trained separately for this task; their load misses do not establish
a new official hackathon score or rule out better supervised models.
Chen's first-stop adapter is tested only on persistent lines under DC and
white-injection assumptions.

\subsection{Broader Hackathon Scope and Retrospective Transfer}
The earlier all-family pipeline and integrity interface are retained as
separate results. An archived observation-only composition reproduces
all \SystemWindows{} physical/integrity windows and reconstructs
availability over \SystemReports{} reports; this verifies interface
consistency, not additional detection accuracy. On retrospectively
annotated RAW0001 windows, a source-only learned hybrid changes joint
family/source correctness from \RawZIPJoint{}/\RawZIPTotal{} to
\RawHybridJoint{}/\RawHybridTotal{}, with one gain and no losses. It is
not the physical bank and does not establish broad transfer. The frozen
physical bank rejects that record because the prefix ROCOF noise scale
is outside its admitted support; no later quiet prefix was substituted
to force admission.
The supplement preserves these outcomes alongside the simulation results.

\subsection{Simulation and Deployment Boundary}
The causal catalog has nine load buses and nine lines, with a quiet
startup. Missing PMUs, unknown families, unrestricted simultaneous
sources, unseen-source transfer and field records remain untested. The
generating plant and inference predictors share the F01 equation family
and PMU map. The three R/X conditions perturb parameters within that
family; they do not test a different producer, control law or instrument
installation. Thus the high within-simulator hit rate is not evidence of
the same rate on RAW0001 or a field network. The
simulator keeps a provisional BUS5 current terminal and omits BUS39
current angle. Its noise includes temporal and cross-PMU dependence
within signal families but omits the archived cross-family factor;
frequency/ROCOF noise is added after phase-based estimation as separate
signal families. It is not obtained by differentiating the exported noisy
angle, and the archived cross-family noise factor is not applied. Hence agreement with
this simulator does not verify the source producer's CT placement or a
complete physical PMU installation.

Out-of-bank R/X preserves most timely source decisions but sharply raises
unmatched alarms. The parent-cluster intervals describe these simulated
parents, not all grids or PMU layouts. Context fitting, history pruning
and shared covariance approximations also prevent a calibrated-posterior
claim. Report-causal operation has not been timed as a real-time service;
the implementation is not validated for protection or control.
