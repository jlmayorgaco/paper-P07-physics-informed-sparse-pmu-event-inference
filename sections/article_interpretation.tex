\section{Discussion}
\label{sec:discussion}

\subsection{What State Continuity Explains}
A restored operator can leave a nonzero physical state, so returning
the predictor to equilibrium introduces a residual that another
candidate can explain. The reset control shows this failure at fixed
thresholds; bounded profiling and marginalization tie in that control.
The nominal signature diagnostics help explain short delays under the
assumed noise model. They do not predict operational error rates.
The bank has no online applicability gate to reject decisions after a plant
change invalidates its models. Sparse-PMU transient-stability
work has reported an explicit self-check for that applicability question
\cite{wang2023sparsepmutsa}. The present signature diagnostics were evaluated retrospectively and do
not suppress alarms.

\subsection{Coverage, Alarm Cost and Scope}
In the held-out comparison, the history-bank procedure had higher
timely coverage and fewer event-record extra alarms than the continuous GLR.
ExtraTrees emits fewer extra alarms but misses more interventions.
These are contrasts between complete procedures calibrated to a common
normal-alarm target; zero observed normal alarms give upper bounds, not
equality of the underlying rates. Under an adequate Markov model, the full
augmented-state distribution is sufficient for prediction. Compressing
histories to their mean can discard covariance and distinct state
alternatives. A history-bank advantage over that compression would therefore
concern a practical representation of uncertainty, rather than information
unavailable to every state observer. The supplementary distributional
analysis separates predictive sufficiency from historical cause attribution.
The present comparison does not isolate these effects.

\subsection{Transfer and Measurement Limits}
Observations and predictors use the same simulator family. The catalog
is fixed, the gaps between interventions are 3--8\,s, and no intervention
overlaps another. Transfer of the dynamic bank to a distinct plant,
shorter gaps and unseen sources are untested. Under independent per-PMU
noise at \RevNoiseAddedRmsLow{}\% added RMS TVE, the bank misses most load changes. This limits transfer
beyond the calibrated measurement model. The terminal map retains the provisional bus-5 current
terminal and excludes the bus-39 current angle. A dropout with held
observations prevents recovery in the
auxiliary stress test. The bank did not start on the retrospective
competition record; the integrity checks and separate attributor in the
supplement do not validate the dynamic bank on that record.

The tested IEEE-39 plant also has no grid-forming inverter controller
dynamics. Applying the method to converter-dominated networks would require
adding the relevant control and current-limiting states and testing those
predictors anew; GFMI surveys describe several such modeling choices
\cite{rathnayake2021gridforming}.

\subsection{Approximation and Processing Cost}
Pruning, steady-state covariances, startup fitting and compression
make the attribution weights approximate. Retrospective proposals
change inner history weights without updating the outer model weights.
The nominal bank processed reports faster than they arrived in a
measured replay on one thread; the five-model version did not.
This timing is specific to the reported hardware and records.

\subsection{Adaptation Without Absorbing an Intervention}
Adaptive noise estimation has already been studied on IEEE-39
\cite{wang2019adaptiveh}. Supplementary nominal-operator diagnostics support
retaining recovery covariance when onset and amplitude are known. However,
continuous noise adaptation did not yield a conclusive attribution gain,
and a quiet-history gate rarely admitted updates. These tests use a local
likelihood scan, not the reported history bank; they motivate covariance
propagation but do not validate adaptive event inference. The supplement
specifies that extension and separates it from cyber analysis, which needs
its own alerts and validation. Missing reports or physical residuals alone
do not identify an attack.
