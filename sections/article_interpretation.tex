\section{Discussion}
\label{sec:discussion}

\subsection{What the Comparisons Establish}

The reset experiment shows that discarding recovery state can turn genuine
system motion into an apparent new event. The nominal signature analysis
explains the mechanism: a reset leaves an innovation that a new-event
candidate can fit. The size of this innovation alone does not fix the
probability of an unmatched alarm because the bank still applies its
proposal and publication rules.

The common-budget comparison asks a different question. The history bank
reports more correct diagnoses before OFF than either comparator. Its
unmatched alarm count falls between the GLR and ExtraTrees counts. The GLR
retains a continuous state but also differs in onset search, score and
commitment. The comparison thus measures the effect of the entire
procedure. If the augmented state and transition model are sufficient for
prediction, the full conditional state distribution captures the effects
of prior histories. A mean alone can lose covariance and distinct
alternatives. A controlled comparison that preserves the mean is needed
to separate state carry from the benefit of retaining alternatives.

\subsection{Transfer and Measurement Limits}
Observations and predictors use the same simulator family. The catalog
is fixed, the gaps between interventions are 3--8\,s, and no intervention
overlaps another. Transfer of the dynamic bank to a distinct plant,
shorter gaps and unseen sources are untested. Under independent per-PMU
noise at \RevNoiseAddedRmsLow{}\% added RMS TVE, the bank misses most load changes. This limits transfer
beyond the calibrated measurement model. The terminal map retains the provisional bus-5 current
terminal and excludes the bus-39 current angle. A dropout with held
observations prevents recovery in the
auxiliary stress test. The bank did not start on the retrospective
competition record; the integrity checks and separate attributor in the
supplement do not validate the dynamic bank on that record.

The tested IEEE-39 plant also has no grid-forming inverter controller
dynamics. Applying the method to converter-dominated networks would require
adding the relevant control and current-limiting states and testing those
predictors anew; GFMI surveys describe several such modeling choices
\cite{rathnayake2021gridforming}.

\subsection{Approximation and Processing Cost}
Pruning, steady-state covariances, startup fitting and compression
make the attribution weights approximate. Retrospective proposals
change inner history weights without updating the outer model weights.
The nominal bank processed reports faster than they arrived in a
measured replay on one thread; the five-model version did not.
This timing is specific to the reported hardware and records.
