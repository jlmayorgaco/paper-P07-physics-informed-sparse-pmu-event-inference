\section{Related Work}
\label{sec:narrative-related}

\subsection{Change Detection and Isolation in Dynamic Systems}
Sequential detection with a prior on the change time leads to the
Shiryaev statistic, whose posterior-probability threshold is optimal for
a geometric change-time prior \cite{shiryaev1963optimum}. Detection
\emph{and isolation} among several post-change laws was formalized by
Nikiforov \cite{nikiforov1995generalized} and Lai
\cite{lai2000sequential}; asymptotically, the delay for a given error
level is inversely proportional to the Kullback--Leibler information
separating the true post-change law from its competitors
\cite{tartakovsky2014sequential}. For linear state-space systems, Willsky
and Jones introduced signatures of jumps propagated through a Kalman
filter and estimated their magnitude by generalized likelihood ratios
\cite{willsky1976glr}. Separating a constant bias from the state, as
Friedland did for recursive filtering \cite{friedland1969bias}, gives the
affine state--severity representation used in our load histories.
Interacting and multiple-hypothesis filters propagate mode-conditioned
states and mode-sequence histories with pruning and merging
\cite{blom1988imm,boers2005m3h}. Our method inherits these mechanisms. Its
post-change models, however, are switched DAE operators of a power
network, and a restored intervention remains a competing cause of later
observations.

\subsection{Physical Inference of Grid Events}
Unknown-input and DAE estimators recover electrical states despite
incomplete models or exogenous inputs
\cite{gillijns2007input,nugroho2022dae,katanic2024recursive}. Functional
observability and descriptor-model diagnosability distinguish recovering a
state quantity from identifying the input that produced it
\cite{fernando2010functional,montanari2022functional,eriksson2013diagnosability,scharf1994matched}.
Phasor-angle hypotheses support outage identification
\cite{tate2008outage}, Bayesian outage inference with uncertain states
\cite{zhao2014outages}, and quickest line-outage detection
\cite{chen2016quickest,rovatsos2017outage}. Yang \emph{et al.} add a time-varying AC likelihood chart \cite{yang2020control} and particle-filter generator states \cite{yang2021particle} for persistent line outages, without restoration or loads. Anguluri \emph{et al.} recover
sparse forcing locations and inputs of linear dynamics by group LASSO
\cite{anguluri2023group}, and sensor placement determines which sources
are distinguishable \cite{jamei2020limits,conte2023fault,yildiz2025sparse}.
Warner \emph{et al.} compare simulated voltage/current fault trajectories
over a moving onset window \cite{warner2023highfidelity} and derive
error/delay guarantees for an adaptive matrix CuSum
\cite{warner2024matrix}. Trajectory sensitivities and Gaussian evidence
have been combined for parameter inference in power-system DAEs
\cite{nagi2022trajectory,albustami2026dae}. PMU-based nodal-inertia
estimation extends this neighboring line of work to spatially resolved
dynamic parameters under ambient and transient conditions
\cite{brahma2026nodal}. State and parameter estimates do not by themselves
identify the intervention that produced them. Here the
DAE-derived signatures provide a profiled separation diagnostic at a
supplied onset, which we compare retrospectively with attribution
outcomes; it is not a general detectability guarantee.
Le \emph{et al.} recently proposed a physics-informed graph neural
operator that maps sparse, intermittent PMU windows to unmeasured rotor
angle and speed trajectories, with swing equations in the decoder and a
learned correction for model mismatch \cite{le2026sparsegno}. That
preprint addresses dynamic state reconstruction. Our method identifies
which intervention explains observations while an earlier response is
still evolving.

\subsection{Learned Diagnosis From Sparse Measurements}
Learned and knowledge-driven representations identify PMU disturbances
and assets \cite{pandey2020realtime,li2024knowledge,yildiz2024few}.
For sparse-PMU transient-stability assessment, Wang \emph{et al.} combine
dominant-bus selection with a bidirectional recurrent assessor and an
online check of model applicability under system changes
\cite{wang2023sparsepmutsa}. This is a useful precedent for monitoring
whether a learned assessment remains valid, but its output is a stability
assessment rather than a source attribution for successive physical
events.
Barati's gated-recurrent-unit model predicts disruptions from historical
synchrophasor streams, a forecasting task distinct from assigning an
observed intervention to a physical source during recovery
\cite{barati2019faster}.
SHRED reconstructs IEEE-39 dynamic states from a few PMUs
\cite{pomarico2026shred}, and Dey \emph{et al.} localize distribution
topology changes with hardware-in-the-loop validation
\cite{dey2026topology}. Graph neural networks encode the grid topology in the
learned map, for event identification \cite{yuan2023gnn}, physics-informed
state estimation \cite{ngo2024pignn} and localization after reconstructing
unmeasured trajectories \cite{rimkus2025gnn}. These applications span state reconstruction and event diagnosis. In the
present attribution task, the physical bank derives candidate responses
from the network equations. Its signature analysis describes separation
under the assumed plant and noise model. ExtraTrees provides a learned
comparison on the same 18-source catalog.

\subsection{Measurement Integrity}
Bad-data detection in state estimation tests residuals against the
redundancy of a network model \cite{abur2004state}; analytical-redundancy
parity relations generalize this to dynamic systems
\cite{chow1984analytical}. For synchrophasors, robust estimators separate
outliers from excursions \cite{chatterjee2020robust}, utility data show
missing, locked, quantized and time-shifted reports \cite{biswas2025quality},
and attack identification characterizes which corrupted sensor sets remain
distinguishable from system behavior \cite{pasqualetti2013attack}. Recent
P-class synchrophasor-estimation work optimizes front-end accuracy and
latency under IEC/IEEE measurement requirements
\cite{islam2026sogi,iec2018synchrophasor}; a review of PLL-based
synchronization also relates front-end phase tracking to PMU applications
in distributed generation \cite{adetayo2026pllpmu}. End-to-end PMU tests
under fault transients quantify how estimator errors affect a downstream
application \cite{becejac2019pmu}. Multisnapshot state estimation models
time-correlated instrument-transformer errors together with line-parameter
uncertainty \cite{dantona2026multisnapshot}; IEC~61850-9-2 co-simulation
has also been used to assess OpenPMU and virtual-PMU state estimation
\cite{celeita2023iec61850}. These contributions address measurement
generation, measurement uncertainty or state estimation. Our bank starts from
reported phasor channels and does not validate a PMU estimator or a
hardware-in-the-loop interface. The two parity relations used in the
supplement require no state estimator and are connected to the information
available for physical attribution when a PMU is lost.
