\section{Reference and Response Are Different Corrections}
\label{sec:ei-x02}

A separate controlled experiment tests a component of the local predictor:
whether correcting the PMU reference is sufficient when operation changes.
It compares a fixed signature, that signature with the measured prefix
offset, and an operating-conditioned signature with the same offset.
All methods receive the load family and ON/OFF times. Conditioning reduces
median relative severity error, evaluated at the generating source, from
\EIRecentSeverity{}\% to \EICondSeverity{}\%, while matching reference-only
source accuracy. Thus better response fidelity does not automatically
improve source decisions, and these oracle-context results are not another
unknown-onset detector score. The supplementary material gives the mixed
derivative, full scenario design, paired results, nominal-condition
counterexample and signal plots. This component experiment motivates
conditional signatures without enlarging the causal stream claim.\AIDraftingCredit{}
