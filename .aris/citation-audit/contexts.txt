\subsection{Trajectory-Based and Sequential Diagnosis}
Warner \emph{et al.} compare simulated voltage/current fault trajectories
over a moving onset window and return candidate lines
\cite{warner2023highfidelity}. Their evaluated target is short-circuit
identification before clearing; load changes are an adverse-event test.
Our target follows load changes and line openings through restoration and
successive interventions. Physical trajectory libraries, remote PMUs and
retrospective onset scanning are shared ingredients. Their procedure was
not run on our data, so no numerical superiority is inferred.

The Adaptive Matrix CuSum derives sequential identification error/delay
results under specified observation laws \cite{warner2024matrix}.
Multiple-model filtering already propagates conditional state/mode
hypotheses \cite{blom1988imm}. The multiple-model multiple-hypothesis

bank size is therefore not an original principle of our method.

KEY: eriksson2013diagnosability
LOCATION: sections/article_information.tex:33
CONTEXT:
A better physical prediction need not produce a better location. Competing
sources may remain similar after their severities and initial conditions
are fitted. For a response $s_h$ at specified severity and context, the
distance to an admitted competitor is
\begin{equation}
 d_h^2=\min_{j\ne h}\ \min_{\beta_j\in\mathcal A_j}
       \|s_h-s_j(\beta_j)\|_{\Sigma^{-1}}^2.
 \label{eq:narrative-separation}
\end{equation}
The set $\mathcal A_j$ fixes allowed signs, severities and nuisance
coordinates. Small distance makes source discrimination noise-sensitive;
it is not a universal error formula or a calibrated decision certificate
\cite{eriksson2013diagnosability,scharf1994matched}.

\begin{abstract}
This supplement gives the executable statistical contract of the causal
physical-history method: local DAE reduction, candidate transitions,
truncated-severity evidence, state updates, restoration, worked examples,
and scenario-level reproduction. It also defines the measurement-integrity
interface and separates causal alarm, diagnosis and restoration metrics
from the historical hackathon window evaluation. Earlier reconstruction,
batch-source and proposed joint-estimator studies are preserved in the
separately compiled Historical Technical Notes. Their results do not enter
the causal method's denominators.
\end{abstract}

phasor measurement units, physical event attribution, measurement integrity,
causal inference, reproducibility.

The article retains the physical model, the defining evidence integral,
the recovery mechanism, the decision outputs and decisive results. This
supplement supplies the intermediate calculations. The Historical Technical
Notes develop functional observability, nuisance-profiled information and
source ambiguity, and distinguish the implemented batch estimator $\mathcal E_{\mathrm B}$ from
the proposed joint estimator $\mathcal E_{\mathrm J}$. Neither should be
silently substituted for the causal history bank, archived as R11.
The conditional Gaussian updates use standard filtering identities
\cite{sarkka2013bayesian}; the derivation below specifies their role and
the approximations made by the implemented bank.
