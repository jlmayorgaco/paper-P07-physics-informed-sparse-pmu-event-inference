\section{Measurement Integrity and the Historical Submission Interface}
\label{app:integrity-interface}

An electrical intervention and a measurement failure are separate objects.
The physical output identifies a family and asset; the integrity output
identifies missing or corrupt PMU observations. Missingness alone does not
establish malicious intent. A concurrent physical event and missing PMU can
therefore have two different locations.\AIDraftingCredit{}

The successor interface reuses the fitted detector, classifier and localizer
from the actual submitted archive, rather than a similarly named exploratory
pipeline. Archive member hashes are checked before inference. The input
schema admits timestamps, three-phase voltage/current magnitudes and angles,
frequency, ROCOF and the observed availability flag. Event labels, locations
and annotated ON/OFF times are excluded. The fitted feature lists are also
checked for label fields. This distinction matters because a historical
diagnostic utility estimated a baseline from labeled normal samples over
the complete record; that utility is not part of this inference interface.

The submitted classifier uses a physical-family head, a bad-data head and a
missing/composition head with fixed rules. Its E5 and E7 outputs refer to
missing and bad-data PMUs, respectively. E6 denotes missing data together
with a physical prediction; its legacy location is the physical source,
not the missing PMU. In the archived configuration, both composition and
physical-confidence thresholds are zero, so E6 requires a nonzero physical
prediction in a window with missing data; it is not a calibrated test of
concurrent causation. E8 is the legacy mixed-physical proxy class, not an
open-set rejection guarantee.

The availability interface evaluates each received report without looking
ahead. It retains the affected PMU and channel mask rather than replacing
missing measurements with future samples. The historical bad-data classifier
uses a complete window and reports its result at the end. Within-window
interpolation and centered features are permitted only under that delayed
decision contract. Broadcasting the result to earlier timestamps does not
make those earlier labels causal. The inherited cyber/integrity component
is a comparator and integration component, not a newly claimed detector.

The implemented composition has two temporal scopes. Received-report
availability messages update the current missing-PMU state. Completed-window
records describe only their observed intervals; they retain separate physical
and integrity fields and cannot assert current corruption or physical
restoration. Their joint emission is the later component emission, with each
original time retained. For example, a window that contains missing data can
finish after the PMU has resumed reporting; publishing that interval must not
reopen the missing episode. Likewise, an earlier ML source diagnosis does not
advance the submitted classifier's window-completion time.

The sealed stream contains \SystemMessages{} messages, including
\SystemWindows{} completed windows. Post-seal projection checks reproduce
the physical source, integrity diagnosis, source-selection provenance and
unavailable states of the components, and recover current availability on
all \SystemReports{} reports without disagreement. The comparison retains
the original component evaluations. It neither reruns inference nor creates
a combined accuracy from report-level missingness and window-level physical
diagnosis. The frozen physical filter remains explicitly unavailable.

\section{Alarm, Diagnosis and Restoration Are Different Decisions}
\label{app:decision-metrics}

Let $\tau$ and $\omega$ be true ON and OFF, and let
$t_A$, $t_D$ and $t_R$ denote the emission times of an alarm, source
diagnosis and restoration. The detector can signal before it has a source.
An eventual correct source at $t_D>t_A$ is not a correct location already
available at $t_A$. Timely attribution requires a correct family and source
at $t_D<\omega$. Estimated onset times never replace emission times in a
delay metric.\AIDraftingCredit{}

An event is associated with the first eligible unmatched alarm using time,
before inspecting the predicted label. Incorrect labels remain associated
errors rather than being reassigned to another event to improve the score.
An alarm without a diagnosis, a wrong diagnosis, and no alarm are distinct
outcomes. Restricted delay includes failures up to the evaluation horizon;
conditional delay among successes is reported separately.

A restoration of an unmatched episode and a premature restoration of a
matched event are also different errors. With a tolerance $\delta$ fixed
before scoring, premature restoration is $t_R<\omega-\delta$; its lost
active duration is $[\omega-t_R]_+$. Signed restoration delay is
$t_R-\omega$. Report its distribution, missing closures and episodes still
open at the horizon; a median alone can hide early closures.

The original C01 evaluator is preserved. Its false-restoration
counter counts restorations of unmatched episodes, and its
first-correct field can use the diagnosis associated with the
first alarm even when emitted later. The successor evaluation reports these
quantities with their restricted meanings and adds explicit premature
closure and joint-decision metrics. This is a correction of evaluation
semantics, not a change to the frozen detector or evidence that either
failure occurred in a particular C01 record.

\section{Retrospective RAW Comparison and Independent Units}
\label{app:raw-contract}

The archived RAW0001 table contains 21 contexts, including nine labeled-normal
and twelve abnormal contexts. Its recovered loader places a 30-s window
three seconds before a supplied abnormal onset, and selects normal contexts
from supplied normal intervals. Even a successful reproduction would measure
classification and localization within event-guided contexts, not continuous
detection. However, the recovered calendar is incompatible with the event
annotations in the actual RAW files. File-history checks show that those
RAW files have not changed, and the timing differences cannot be corrected
by a common offset. The archived score is therefore not a reproduced
comparison endpoint. The unsuccessful reconstruction and its provenance
audit are retained; predictions from mismatched windows are not counted as
accuracy against the archived labels.\AIDraftingCredit{}

A separate complete-record replay uses fixed windows without event-guided
placement and retains all normal, physical, missing and corrupt periods.
Both interfaces preserve availability failures and abstentions. Physical
and integrity locations are scored separately where their meanings differ;
the legacy single-label export is retained for comparison. Physical-source
scoring additionally requires verified source annotations; an event-code
sequence alone cannot identify every intervention boundary or source.
The separately sealed successor analysis uses the recovered original
source workbook for physical identity, retaining current event-code
support for window membership. Its coarse timing does not resolve the
individual actuation or restoration times.
This replay is not an official competition score. RAW0001 previously informed F01
calibration, so even a frozen new replay remains retrospective.

Simulation partitions use the complete physical parent and all of its
derived windows, conditions and noise realizations. Validation belongs
inside the development fraction. Repartitioning previously inspected
campaigns does not restore independent-test status. Uncertainty calculations
resample physical parents, retaining their paired conditions together.
Different PMU channels and adjacent 30-Hz reports are not independent trials.

\section{Repository Boundaries and Reproduction}

The article and current supplement compile independently from
\texttt{main.tex} and \texttt{supplement.tex}. The separate
\texttt{historical\_notes.tex} preserves corrected geometry and evidence
theorems, earlier state reconstruction, the historical batch estimator,
the proposed joint estimator and their original empirical qualifications.
Moving this material does not remove negative results or upgrade an
unexecuted estimator to an implemented one.\AIDraftingCredit{}

The submission successor is versioned in the research repository under
\nolinkurl{experiments/PAPER_SUBMISSION_S01/}. It contains the source
provenance, observation-only interface, metric specification, synthetic
regressions, and separate historical-window and complete-record outputs.
Performance tables enter the manuscript only after prediction sealing and
evaluation. A successful code check or PDF build is not itself a performance
result.
