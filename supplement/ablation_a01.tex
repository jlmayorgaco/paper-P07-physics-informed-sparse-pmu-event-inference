\section{Controlled Component Ablation}
\label{app:a01}
The frozen protocol and all output tables are supplied in the source archive under generated/frozen/ablation\_a01\_20261102. Each of four arms accounts for all 24 intended records (18 continuous, three normal and three persistent): the evaluation audit records 96/96 available outputs and zero failures. The trial is posthoc and no score threshold is calibrated to these outcomes. Each normal control is a 600-s record with 588 s of evaluated exposure after startup, for 1,764 s total.
