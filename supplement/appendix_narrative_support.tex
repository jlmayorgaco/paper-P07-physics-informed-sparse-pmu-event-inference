\subsection{The Causal Implementation's Local Amplitude Integral}

This calculation describes the retained local Gaussian history model used by R11, not a replacement for the batch estimator's GH31 quadrature. For one load-history interval, write its new-report residual as
\[
 e=s a+\epsilon,\qquad \epsilon\sim\mathcal N(0,S),\qquad
 a\sim\operatorname{TN}_{[l,u]}(\mu,v).
\]
The physical transition/observation operators supply $s$; conditional state and colored-noise propagation supply $S$. Here $\mu,v$ parameterize the underlying untruncated normal density, not its bounded mean and variance. Define
\[
 g=s^\top S^{-1}s,\quad q=s^\top S^{-1}e,\quad
 v'=\frac{v}{1+vg},\quad \mu'=\mu+v'(q-\mu g),
\]
and $Z(\mu,v)=\Phi((u-\mu)/\sqrt v)-\Phi((l-\mu)/\sqrt v)$.
Completing the square gives
\begin{align*}
 \log p(e\mid h)={}&-\tfrac12(e^\top S^{-1}e+\log|S|+n_y\log(2\pi))\\
 &+\tfrac12\left[\log(v'/v)+\mu'^2/v'-\mu^2/v\right]\\
 &+\log Z(\mu',v')-\log Z(\mu,v).
\end{align*}
Reported amplitude moments use the corresponding truncated-normal moments. The integral is evaluated over the retained amplitude intervals; it is not replaced by the best-fitting amplitude alone. Fixed line-mode hypotheses use their switched operator without a load-amplitude integral.

The square completion is standard. Its role here is to show precisely how a physical sensitivity and an uncertain intensity enter the executable likelihood. It is exact for this conditional Gaussian model; linearization, covariance sharing, nuisance substitution and history pruning prevent an exact unrestricted nonlinear-posterior claim. The frozen implementation is \texttt{integrated\_loglike} in the R11 \texttt{observer/static\_rx.py}; the one-report model weights call it before current-report screening. Rolling proposals do not create a second copy of the same observations.
