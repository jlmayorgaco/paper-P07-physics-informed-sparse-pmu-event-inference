\section{Executable Single-State Comparison}
\label{app:single-state}

This control uses the same conditional nominal plant and measurement
operators as the nominal history bank, but retains only one committed mode and its
augmented conditional state. Temporary alternatives live for at most
19 reports. The five-model history bank additionally mixes resistance and
reactance alternatives. Proposals begin at report index 300 and publication
at index 360, with zero-based indices. Each available report after proposal
startup starts two subframe transition candidates
for every admitted source when inactive, or two OFF candidates for the
committed source when active. Every candidate uses the covariance regime
chosen from preceding reports; the current sample cannot select its own
predictive covariance.\AIDraftingCredit{}

For a load ON alternative, write the innovation as $e_k-a s_k$. The affine
conditional state is $m_k+a d_k$. Accumulate over its contiguous suffix
\begin{align}
 G&=\sum_k s_k^\top S_k^{-1}s_k,\\
 U&=\sum_k s_k^\top S_k^{-1}e_k,\\
 C&=-\frac12\sum_k\left(e_k^\top S_k^{-1}e_k
        +\log|S_k|+p\log(2\pi)\right).
\end{align}
Then $\widehat a=\operatorname{clip}(U/G,-0.2,0.2)$ when $G>0$, with
$\widehat a=0$ for a flat objective. The profile contrast is
\begin{equation}
 \Lambda=C+\widehat aU-\tfrac12\widehat a^2G-C_{\rm ref}.
\end{equation}
Here $C_{\rm ref}$ is the sum of the committed predictor's pre-update
conditional log densities on exactly the same suffix. A line uses its
discrete switched operator, without fitting a load amplitude. The reference
uses its preceding severity estimate for prediction and can refine that
estimate after receiving the new report. A candidate never changes the
reference state before commitment. A load OFF alternative holds its birth
amplitude fixed rather than re-profiling it.

ON scores subtract $\log(18\times2\times19)$; OFF scores subtract
$\log(2\times19)$. These fixed search penalties are heuristics. Maximizing
over overlapping suffixes and bounded amplitudes does not produce a
calibrated posterior or a sequential false-alarm guarantee. For the history
banks, ON uses the log odds of $M(c)$ and OFF the log odds of
$M_{\rm cause}(c)P({\rm OFF}\mid c)$.

The common clock requires the same winning ON source to exceed
$\log99$ for six consecutive monitored reports. It emits alarm and source
diagnosis together. Six qualifying OFF reports for that source close the
episode. A clock-induced commitment selects the candidate's current
conditional state. Its estimate may change through hypothesis selection;
it is never replaced with equilibrium or a zero state. A missing report
propagates the retained mode without measurement correction, clears the
short alternatives and contributes no confirmation evidence. The H04
comparison contains no such post-startup missing observations.

After OFF, the preceding discrepancy regime persists until 60 consecutive
available, inferred-inactive reports meet the inherited quiet criterion.
Its innovation is computed with the normal operator from the preceding
committed state. A new active episode or missing report clears that quiet
run. Retrospective source labels and event times never enter these steps.

The comparison has one fixed publication clock but different evidence
semantics, search and severity treatment. It therefore compares complete
procedures. It does not establish a matched false-alarm operating point,
nor isolate a causal effect of long-history averaging. The source tree,
execution hashes, every prediction and the successor event metrics are
preserved in the experiment manifest.
