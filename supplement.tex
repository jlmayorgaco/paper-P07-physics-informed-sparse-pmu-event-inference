\begin{abstract}
This supplement documents the implemented recovery-aware history bank:
its local filtering calculations, measurement map, publication rules and
simulation contracts. It records the complete confirmatory outcomes,
controlled severity-score/state-carry ablation and continuous single-state
control. The operating-point signature and recovery-geometry studies keep
their separate, known-onset contracts and denominators. All empirical
results reuse the fixed evidence; no new campaign or threshold selection
is introduced here. Earlier voltage-reconstruction, simultaneous-source
and general diagnosability studies are preserved in the separately
compiled Historical Technical Notes.
An explicitly prospective section specifies an adaptive extension and
its separate cyber interface; its small-model checks are not campaign results.
\end{abstract}
\begin{keywords}
phasor measurement units, sequential diagnosis, physical state continuity,
conditional filtering, reproducibility.

\paragraph{Exposure convention for the ablation alarm rate.}
The ablation table reports extras per event-hour with the complete
120-s event record, including startup, as denominator. Its continuous exposure is
$18\times120=2160$ s. The main article's confirmatory alarm-rate figure
instead uses monitored exposure after the 12-s startup. Under that
convention the ablation exposure would be $18\times108=1944$ s and each
reported rate difference and interval bound would be multiplied by
$120/108$. Counts, event matches and the stored table are unchanged;
these two rate conventions must not be compared without this conversion.
