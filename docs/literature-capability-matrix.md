# Literature capability matrix

## Scope and audit rule

This matrix positions the paper against methods for state estimation, event identification, source localization, partial observation, and measurement integrity. Scores from different plants or targets are not treated as a leaderboard. A method is a common-contract baseline only if its observation operator, candidate space, split unit, and source-exclusion rule can be reproduced. ## Gap supported by the matrix

The literature already contains joint state/input estimation, partial-observation reconstruction, sparse-PMU localization, state-plus-outage monitoring, and state-plus-attack localization. The defensible gap is their intersection under a stricter experimental contract: one causal transmission-system model; physical and measurement-integrity states that can coexist; source hypotheses evaluated through physical responses rather than a closed source-label list; a diagnosability quantity linked prospectively to posterior ambiguity; and complete physical-source exclusion from source-labeled fitting and tuning.
