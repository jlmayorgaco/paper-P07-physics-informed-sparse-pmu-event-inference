## Current evidence boundary

The prospective result is strong for moderate and finite simultaneous load interventions and weak for weak--weak exact support. The positive T120 equilibrium margin rules out equilibrium-degenerate pairs only in the validated local IEEE 39-bus model. Near-unit signature coherence exposes severe conditioning, and the 32-path T120 physical validation covers only one nominal operating point. These results do not establish a universal absence of ambiguity or a multi-family joint estimator.
