## Forbidden upgrades

- Do not describe the manuscript as an end-to-end validated multi-family joint estimator.
- Do not call the legacy event model zero-shot, source-generalizing, Bayesian, or robust.
- Do not call retrospective inference real-time or continuous unless latency and normal exposure are measured.
- Do not call U2 or the recentering curvature covariance fully calibrated.
- Do not infer topology, PMU loss/join, cross-simulator, field, or HIL performance from the state or load-source evidence.
- Do not call the numerical load tangent analytic, or promote the inconclusive projected-Fisher result into a source-confusion law.
- Do not claim that the production callback already returns the physical algebraic right limit until callback/right-limit logging and derivative-convergence tests are frozen.
- Do not present the $\gamma_1$ law as empirically validated until the profiled nonlinear distance divided by $a^2$ converges under a preregistered amplitude grid.
- Do not use an unconstrained subspace margin as the exact physical margin when sign, minimum-magnitude, or duration constraints are active at the optimum.
- Do not claim shared-source amplitude independence for one-sided boundary events unless the relaxed optimum satisfies the physical constraints.
- Do not turn span intersection, a zero principal angle, or `spark <= 2K` into one-sided physical ambiguity without a feasible cone-intersection witness.
- Do not claim that profiled distance alone identifies cardinality across nested supports; the zero-amplitude closure makes that criterion degenerate.
- Do not use regular interior Laplace or chi-square asymptotics at a one-sided or singular boundary without the corresponding cone or higher-order correction.
- Do not replace an exactly redundant parameter with a Hessian pseudodeterminant; integrate on the identifiable quotient and induce the prior on the reduced coordinate.
- Do not turn weak Fisher information, a curvature-gate flag, or near-collinearity in the RAWSIM39 ledger into a proved second-order or exactly redundant direction.
- Do not quote the simple Gaussian $\Phi(-d/2)$ error formula for amplitude mixtures, unequal covariances, or the full multicandidate posterior.
- Do not claim exact support recovery uniformly to zero amplitude; declare a beta-min or severity contract, and treat Fano bounds as necessary rather than sufficient.
- Do not cite the inaccessible controlled-validation CSV or figures as evidence. Only the independently regenerated toy checks are currently auditable.
- Do not use the unreconciled absolute T120 separation values or turn positive equilibrium margins into a universal identifiability claim.
- Do not describe the retrospective weak-regime predictor as prospectively validated or claim a curvature-dominated mechanism; the tested directions were first-order resolvable.
- Do not present utility-only discrepancy optimization, its infinite-variance limit, or an unexecuted SDP/SOS hierarchy as a tuned Bayesian estimator or IEEE 39-bus result.
- Do not turn oracle recentering into a deployable-method claim.
- Do not claim exact functional identifiability when the projected residual is nonzero.
