## Keep the experiments and calibrations distinct

- **Primary history-bank comparison:** one nominal predictor bank; thresholds selected on 12 event and 24 normal calibration trajectories under a normal-alarm bound of at most one alarm per monitored normal hour. The test had 18 event and 24 normal trajectories. Bank and GLR thresholds were 6.9068; ExtraTrees used 2.1972 on its own score scale. The zero-normal-alarm result is based on 3.92 hours, with a one-sided 95% upper bound of 0.76 alarms/hour.
- **Recovery-representation experiment:** separate thresholds selected on 36 event and 36 normal reference-plant calibration trajectories. The held-out test used 36 event and 36 normal trajectories **per plant**. History and mean used 6.9068; reset used 5.2933. Each plant’s 5.88 normal hours had zero alarms (upper bound 0.51/hour).
- **Fixed-threshold continuity replay:** retrospective comparison on 18 reference-plant trajectories, with thresholds, observations, and five network hypotheses held fixed. It is not the calibrated two-plant comparison.
- **Network-mismatch and noise studies:** separate replays with their own exposure and admission conditions. In particular, the original startup limits rejected every independent-noise stress record; the widened startup range was a separate replay. Do not combine their results with the calibrated primary comparison.
- **Frozen window comparisons:** the paired composite test, held-out physical-site test, and prospective electrical-localizer test each have different cohorts, protocols, and conclusions. None is the primary history-bank experiment, and none evaluates the hidden competition records.

A concise revision should make these boundaries visible in the abstract, results, and discussion. It should also qualify “novelty” as the recovery-aware application and evaluation, rather than presenting established filter components or the window experiments as proof of general source localization.
