\subsection{Evaluated Domain Profile}

\label{app:domain-profile}

The confirmatory evaluation covers 18 continuous six-event parent schedules, repeated under three network conditions and two standard-deviation noise scales, for 108 120-s event records per method (3.6 event-record hours). It includes 12 normal parents under the same condition/noise crossing, giving 72 600-s records (12.0 planned normal hours). A separate persistent-line lane has nine line sources with three repetitions each, crossed with the same network/noise settings. The twelve-second monitoring warmup follows a ten-second context fit; the earliest scheduled intervention is at 16 s.

Across the 18 unique continuous schedules, finite event duration ranges from 2.02 to 4.95 s. Load changes multiply both active and reactive setpoints by $1+a$ under the frozen impedance law; the prespecified $|a|$ range is 0.03--0.14 (realized signed schedule values range -0.138 to 0.135). Line events are full branch openings followed by restoration. The three network conditions are MATCH, RX\_IN\_SUPPORT and RX\_OUT\_SUPPORT; out-of-support cases use independently signed 1--3% branch factors with R and X paired, fixed before the trajectory and solved at equilibrium. The estimator bank remains fixed at nominal, global $\pm2\%$, and selected-branch $\pm3\%$ paired R/X alternatives.

Noise scales 1 and 2 are standard-deviation multipliers of the frozen R5C.2 stream, with the same seed paired across conditions and scales. Event extras retain the full 120-s event-record denominator; this is not the monitored post-warmup duration. Parent schedules, not repeated condition/noise runs or PMU samples, are the independent unit. The catalog contains nine candidate loads and nine candidate lines; it is not an unseen-source or all-39-bus evaluation.
