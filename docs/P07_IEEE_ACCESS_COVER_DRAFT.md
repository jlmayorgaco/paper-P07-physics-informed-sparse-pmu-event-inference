Dear Editor,

Please consider “Recovery-Aware Physical Attribution of Load and Line
Events From Sparse PMUs” as a Research Article for IEEE Access. The
paper addresses a practical ambiguity: after a load is restored or a line
is reclosed, the remaining transient can resemble a new intervention.
The proposed observer uses measurements from eight PMUs and switched
differential-algebraic predictors to distinguish these responses.

The evaluation measures whether the event family and source can be
reported before restoration. It reports unmatched alarms, monitored
normal exposure and paired uncertainty alongside coverage. The
manuscript explains the scope of the simulated evidence and provides
reproducibility materials with the supplement.

Sincerely,

Jorge Luis Mayorga Taborda
Corresponding author
