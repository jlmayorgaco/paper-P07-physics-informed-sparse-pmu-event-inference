# Paired spatial localization test

Target point estimates are at least 0.96 for nine-class macro-F1, exact family accuracy and exact source accuracy. The comparator is the frozen prefix-scaled response bank. Score one decision per 30-s window after a 60-s quiet prefix. Keep all nonnormal windows in the location denominator, including missed events. Test 24 new records from each of nine M9 templates, using three candidate assets per physical family, variable event onset, duration and severity, and rotated integrity sites. Add 48 quiet records solely to the false-alarm comparison. Report paired uncertainty and per-class errors.

The successor detects the configured class-8 composite when a sustained common current-magnitude change on one PMU overlaps an angle encoding change on another. A whole turn represents the same electrical angle. This rule depends on the raw instrument encoding and recognizes one known composite configuration. It does not test general open-set recognition. No affected PMU identity is used as a source label.

For generation and load, fit the spatial displacement of voltage and current magnitudes and angles after the intervention. For the composite, use the shorter coincident pulse and fit a quiet oscillatory baseline using frequencies estimated at startup. Mask current magnitude at the PMU with the gain excursion. Within each predicted family choose the candidate with the smallest covariance-weighted residual after profiling a positive amplitude. Estimate source signatures and a pooled shrinkage covariance exclusively in development. The previous bank continues to handle line and fault localization. All features use measurements available by window close.

Fit uses 216 new development records and 150 additional records of the known composite template. The historical 108-record development set is a diagnostic set. The new primary and quiet test seeds are 85773209 and 34813171. Save predictions and their SHA-256 in a separate process before scoring. Templates, catalog, instrument conventions and simulator are shared across training and test. M9 spatial attenuation depends on numeric bus-ID proximity; results on these registered templates do not establish performance under different grid physics, on unseen assets or on the competition's hidden dataset.

On the primary cohort, macro-F1 increased from 0.8450 to 0.9907, correct family from 190/216 to 214/216, and exact source from 166/192 to 188/192. Both methods produced zero alarms on 72 quiet windows. All net gains came from the configured composite. Two isolated integrity events were missed and two composites were assigned the wrong source. Converting angles to their signed principal range removed the composite flag in all 24 composite records. This diagnostic tested the flag alone.
