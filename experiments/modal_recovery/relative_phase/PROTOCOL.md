# Paired relative-phase experiment on second switches

**Question.** Does adding angular information from the existing eight PMUs to the frozen recovery-conditioned magnitude detector recover load switches at bus 31? The benchmark is the existing IEEE-39 P2 simulator, its archived CT mapping and noise process, a known first restoration time, and the same catalog of nine loads and nine lines. The published comparison concerns a second event during recovery. Assets, operating point and PMU placement are shared across splits. The instrument model uses the archived terminal orientation, polarity, gain and phase bias; several physical CT identifications remain unverified in the source archive.

**Observation.** Preserve the original eight voltage magnitudes and eight branch-current magnitudes. Form seven voltage angle differences relative to PMU 2 and eight branch-current angle differences relative to the voltage phasor at the same PMU. The latter is an instrument-pair reference even when the archived current terminal is remote from the voltage bus. For each relative angle φ and associated magnitude M use the quadrature component M sin(φ − φ₀), where φ₀ is the circular mean in the first quiet second. A common time-dependent rotation cancels. Current magnitude weights the phase contribution of a lightly loaded or open branch. The transform requires only reports available at the decision time.

**Estimator.** Refit the same recovery-conditioned Gaussian switch likelihood on the augmented observation vector. The recovery and incremental switch templates come from the same network and measurement operator as the magnitude experiment. The posterior for the continuing recovery uses 30 post-restoration reports. Estimate the full feature covariance on 256 recovery-only records, then marginalize over event amplitude and onset age in the original 60-report alarm window and 84-report diagnosis window. The reference, starting time and 29-report diagnosis delay are identical across methods. The magnitude comparator is the published adjusted detector frozen at eae30b1.

**Development.** Resimulate with the archived numerical seeds for the 256 fit records, 320 independent calibration controls and 240 previously disclosed event/control pairs. Require exact equality of the original magnitude channels, clearance and labels. Calibrate on whole-trajectory maxima of the 320 controls and 36 long F01 quiet records. The three threshold candidates are the 313th, 318th and 320th ordered recovery maxima, each bounded below by the maximum long quiet score. Advance a model only if it improves timely bus-31 detection, preserves or improves overall detection, family and exact location, and produces no more control alarms than the magnitude method in development. Among qualifying settings, maximize timely exact location, bus-31 detection, fewer recovery alarms, fewer premature alarms, then the higher threshold.

**Blind comparison.** Freeze source, thresholds, fitted parameters and upstream hashes before generating seed 20977153. Generate 240 independent parent scenarios with 60 in each ordered combination of load and line families. Every positive has a recovery-only twin with the same noise and first event. Each method receives one trace at a time; the generator alone uses the event schedule. Neither the target bus nor a twin is given to inference. Seal both arms' predictions before reading the target file.

For detection, the first alarm must occur within the active second event. For family and exact location, both the first alarm and the decision issued 29 reports later must be within this interval. All three denominators are the 240 scheduled second events. An early alarm forfeits the event. Count false alarms by controls with any alarm out of 240, and separately report early alarms on positive cases and upward crossings over 36 previously published long quiet records. The point target is at least 228 correct on each of the three positive outcomes, fewer than 12 control alarms, and no more alarmed controls than the paired magnitude baseline. Report discordant counts, exact paired tests and marginal one-sided binomial bounds. Equality of observed false-alarm counts is a sample result. The upper bound on the risk of a newly introduced alarm is a conservative upper bound on any absolute increase in control alarm probability.

**Reproduction.** With the upstream science archive and preceding experiment's scratch cohorts available, run `phase_data.py` on fit, cal and dev, then `library.py`, then the fit, quiet and select stages of `development.py`. Run `campaign.py freeze` before `phase_data.py test`. Commit the output of `campaign.py predict` before `campaign.py score`. In the published checkout, `campaign.py verify`, `campaign.py replay` and `campaign.py score` check the freeze, the two arms' per-trace predictions and the endpoint counts. Exclusive creation protects published outputs on rerun.
