# Independent sparse-PMU physical load-source comparison
Before scoring, freeze seed 712490, 24 operating points and all 18 positive-load buses (432 cases). Use the preceding AC hardware layout, eight voltages and six terminal currents, and identical noisy changes for every arm. Draw complex circular differential errors with relative RMS 0.0005 and 0.005 and independent pre-event phasor errors at the same scales. Perturb each operating load independently by up to 7.5 percent. Draw signed event size between 4 and 22 percent; perturb its reactive-to-active change ratio by a factor 0.9–1.1. All candidate sites are available to every estimator, and the family and steady-state windows are given. No detector or site-supervised fitting.
 
Compare the frozen nominal voltage/current projection, a locally updated quadratic response with profiled severity, and the same response integrated over a broad signed severity prior (uniform on signed 2–30 percent). Estimate pre-event load deviations by ridge regression on the noisy sparse pre-event phasors with load-fraction prior standard deviation 0.05. Estimate positive and negative 10 percent source secants from AC solves at that estimated state. Preserve the previous per-signal-family centering and precision weights. Count all hits, gains and losses against the frozen nominal estimator. Report separately from nine-class M9 performance, including regressions.
