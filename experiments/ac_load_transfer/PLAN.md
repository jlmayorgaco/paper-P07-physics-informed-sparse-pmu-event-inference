# Load-location test under nonlinear AC power flow
Before scoring, freeze seed 694031, all 18 positive-load candidates and the eight PMUs. Use RAW bus types, base phasors, constant PQ loads and positive voltage regulation at PV buses. Assign injections consistent with the archived nominal voltage. For each of 16 operating points, perturb each base load independently by up to 5 percent and solve the nonlinear AC equilibrium. At each site impose a signed uniform 5 to 20 percent change of that load at its base power factor and solve again. Compare voltage differences with the frozen impedance dictionary, complex-scaled nominal AC Jacobian columns and real-scaled nominal AC columns. Remove the common spatial offset in all three arms. At each pair of equilibria compare noise-free predictions and the same single draw of circular Gaussian differential phasor error at 0.0005 pu RMS. Include all scheduled sites and disclose solver failures. No parameter fit, site training or M9 labels. This is a synchronous, known-family and known-event-window benchmark under the given topology and controls. It cannot measure nine-class detection or false alarms.
